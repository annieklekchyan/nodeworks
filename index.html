About us

The people behind Nodeworks.

Nodeworks was founded by Ani Klekchyan, a software engineer with more than 10 years in IT. Ani has built mobile apps, web platforms and backend services for companies of all sizes.

Today we combine that experience with the latest AI tools, so ideas become working products faster than ever, without cutting corners. We're based in Yerevan, Armenia, and work with clients all over the world.

FAQ

What can you build?

Websites, online shops, web and mobile apps, AI assistants and automations. Not sure if it's possible? Just ask: chances are it is.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so the price depends on what you need. Tell us about your idea and we'll send you a personal offer.

How long does it take?

A website is usually ready in a few days; bigger apps take a few weeks. You get a clear timeline before we start.

What happens after launch?

We stay with you: updates, new features, backups and fixes. We agree on the format that suits you.

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere, in English, Armenian or Russian.
